About the Body

The body remembers.

The body speaks softly
before it begins to ache.
In tension.
In breath.
In the quiet exhaustion
we learn to ignore.

At Sukha Vihara,
wellness is not correction,
but a gentle return—
to poise,
to softness,
to the wisdom already held
within the body.

Through slow rituals
and intentional touch,
we create moments that invite you
to pause,
release,
and listen again.
